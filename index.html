About us

BAINSA Sapienza is a student-led AI research lab connecting ambitious students with leading researchers, international labs and industry.

  • Culture: A community of like-minded builders. Being a member means more than working on projects: it means discussing research together, sharing experiences, joining international events and building a strong professional network.
  • Methodology: Strong research orientation. Every project is developed with scientific rigor and expert guidance, aiming to publish at the world's leading conferences and collaborate with top global companies.

Divisions

Different divisions. One shared mission.

BAINSA Sapienza is structured into three divisions dedicated to research, outreach and community activities, each contributing to advancing and sharing knowledge:

  • Projects Division
  • Analysis Division
  • Culture Division
